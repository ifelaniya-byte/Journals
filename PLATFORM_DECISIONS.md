# Platform Decisions: what lists where, and why

Verified 2026-08-28. This is the seller's answer sheet: for each platform, which of our
books list, which are held back, at what price, in what order. It overrides nothing the
owner set; it executes it efficiently. Wave sequencing still governs timing (Wave 1 =
KDP only; everything below opens in Wave 2 unless the owner says now).

## The reasoning, platform by platform

### Amazon KDP paperback - everything
All 18 at $9.99, launch four first (Dopamine Menu, 75 Soft, Middle Season, Cozy Corners),
then 5 per week. Create both series pages (Quiet Mind Journals, Quiet Mind Color) early so
every new title strengthens the shelf. Do NOT enable Expanded Distribution: that is
IngramSpark's job in Wave 2, at better wholesale terms.

### Amazon KDP hardcover - journals only
A hardcover earns its price as a keepsake or gift: that is a journal use case. The 7
journal hardcovers (5-Minute Dump $15.99, Settle $14.99, Parallel Lives $14.99, Middle
Season $14.99, Dopamine Menu $14.99, Slow Page $13.99, 75 Soft $13.99) go up in that
order - ads titles first. The 10 coloring hardcovers are HELD: coloring buyers want a
book that lies flat and costs little; a $13.99 case-laminate of the same art as the $9.99
paperback splits the same buyer for a worse product. Release a coloring hardcover only
if a coloring title clearly outsells its print peers. Night Pages cannot have one
(5x8 is not a KDP hardcover trim).

### B&N Press - thick books only
The $14.99 floor is defensible exactly when the book has physical heft. List the 11
titles at 120 pages or more (5-Minute Dump 200, Settle 172, Parallel 160, Middle 160,
Dopamine 150, Slow 144, Fractal 140, Architectural 140, Mosaic 120, Woodland 120,
Night 120). HOLD the seven thin ones (75 Soft 96, First Strokes 100, Easy Garden 100,
Cozy 104, Botanical 104, Celestial 104, Tidal 104): a 96-page book at $14.99 next to
its own $9.99 Amazon listing reads as overpriced, and B&N shoppers comparison-shop.
Free B&N ISBN per title; the 100-title account cap is irrelevant at our size.

### Blurb - the 6 that fit
Blurb trade books come in exactly three trims - 5x8, 6x9, 8x10 (Blurb's own spec,
verified). That admits exactly six of ours: Night Pages (5x8), Settle, Middle Season,
Dopamine Menu, Slow Page, 75 Soft (all 6x9), at $12.99-$13.99 (base + $1). Blurb is the
lowest-priority print platform (premium print costs, modest reach): if we list anything,
start with Middle Season and Settle only, and read the numbers before adding the rest.

### Digital PDF stores - four different jobs
Same prices everywhere ($4.99 attractors x5, $6.99 x12, Middle Season print-only):
- Payhip is the primary direct store (5% fee, unlimited products, EU VAT handled).
- Lemon Squeezy carries the same catalog for international buyers as merchant of record.
- Gumroad leads with the two bundles (Starter Shelf $11.99, Color Complete $39.99) and
  the free samplers - its checkout and email capture suit higher-order, repeat traffic.
- Google Play carries everything including the free samplers (70%, PDF native).
- Ko-fi mirrors Payhip and hosts the samplers for the tip-culture crowd.
Etsy, once the owner okays roughly $3.60 in listing fees: open with the 8 journals -
Etsy's best-selling digital category is printable planners, trackers and self-improvement
downloads, which is exactly what our journals are - plus Cozy Corners and Botanical Ink
(printable-art adjacent). The remaining coloring titles follow only if the first batch
sells. Keep prices identical to the other stores; the fee math slightly disfavors $4.99
on Etsy but consistency is worth more than the ~3% difference.
On every digital listing: cover + listing_02-05 + listing_07 only. The callout image
shows the $9.99 print price and must not appear (warning embedded in every kit).

### What we deliberately do not do
- Whop: wrong marketplace for books.
- Kobo / Apple / StreetLib: no EPUB product exists; reflow would ruin these books.
- Lulu and IngramSpark hardcovers: floors are breakeven; enter only with real margin,
  by owner decision, later.
- No platform gets an automated storefront or posting bot: automated preparation,
  human-gated publication (playbook section 8B).
