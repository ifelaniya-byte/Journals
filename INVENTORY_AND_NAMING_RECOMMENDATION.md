## Best commercial-name direction

### Recommendation: **Calyven Studio**

Use **Calyven Studio** as the first backup/imprint candidate to send to counsel, not as an approved live identity.

Why it is the strongest fit across the portfolio:

- **Broad enough for the inventory:** works for paperbacks, printable cards, a future boxed deck, audio, and wholesale rather than sounding GLP-1-, sleep-, or therapy-specific.
- **Editorial but not restrictive:** `Studio` accommodates books, tactile goods, and digital/audio companions; `Press` or `Paper Co.` would narrow the future product system.
- **Avoids the current root collision:** it does not reuse `Stillwork`, so a negative opinion about the existing recording-business root does not travel with it.
- **More ownable than descriptive alternatives:** it is more distinctive than `Quiet`, `Paper`, `Ritual`, `Calm`, or `Reflection` standing alone. The limited web search did not surface a clear exact-use result, but counsel must still search spelling/phonetic variants (including `Calvin`/`Caly-`), Classes 16/35/41, and common-law/domain use.

It is a flexible descriptive series label for the B10–B18 family, and it reduces the blast radius of a rejected imprint name. It is not automatically a proprietary mark; counsel should decide whether to use it descriptively or clear it as a series name.
