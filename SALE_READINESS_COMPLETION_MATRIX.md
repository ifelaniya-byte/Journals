## 2. Completion definition

A product is only *finished for sale* when all applicable conditions are documented for its **specific edition, format, language, territory, and channel**:

1. **Portfolio:** correct wave/format and, for Wave 2, a signed open-slot decision; for Vault, approval of the actual object/digital test.
2. **Identity:** counsel-cleared name/series/title as needed; approved imprint; buyer-controlled domain only after authorization.
3. **Content:** human cover-to-cover review of prompts, tone, expectation setting, accessibility, interior, cover, metadata, and all customer-facing assets.
4. **Claims/safety:** product-specific counsel/qualified review; no item inherits another SKU’s clearance.
5. **Edition:** correct final source, page count, trim, cover/wrap/dieline, identifier/ISBN where required, and (if selected) QR/audio route.
6. **Price/economics:** authorized list price, current currency/floor/royalty/print-cost calculation, and channel-specific distribution decision.
7. **Proof/platform:** current printer/platform template check plus a human-reviewed physical or digital proof.
8. **Commerce:** named human approves retailer account, tax, privacy, customer-data, refund/fulfilment, and customer-support arrangements.
9. **Release:** named human records `Clear for upload`/equivalent and personally performs the consequential external action.

A green structural validator, a draft package, a translation source digest, a price model row, an AI output, or an inventory-ready file does not satisfy this definition.
