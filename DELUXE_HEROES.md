## Required vendor sequence
1. Send `vendor-rfq-package.md` from the launch kit to three vendors, requesting 250 / 500 / 1,000 quantity breaks and Lunar New Year 2027 capacity details.
2. Compare **landed** unit cost, not factory price. Do not release physical production if cost exceeds target margin.
3. Request component dummy / physical proof from the best two candidates.
4. Place vendor artwork on supplied dielines. Approve paper, pen test, binding, color, foil, closure, box fit, and QR scan from the proof.
5. Only after Gate 3 funding coverage is met, issue a purchase order with signed artwork and packing instructions.

## Do not do
- Do not repurpose KDP cover wraps for a casebound book or magnetic box.
- Do not represent the KDP paperback as containing cards, foil, scent, ribbons, pockets, or audio access that it does not include.
- Do not change card count, page count, finish, or box construction as a stretch goal after production proof without recalculating cost, lead time, and fulfillment.
