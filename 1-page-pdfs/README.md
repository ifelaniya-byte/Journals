# One-Page PDF Products

Two hundred and fifty print-ready, US Letter one-page PDFs — cleaner, more
detailed, and more usable re-imaginings of the proven best-selling one-pagers
found on Etsy, Gumroad, TikTok, Reddit, Pinterest, X, and YouTube. The catalog
is split into seven batches:

- **Batch 1 (1–16)** — proven long-term bestsellers (sales trackers, cheat
  sheets, clinical references, game scorecards).
- **Batch 2 (17–32)** — the categories with the strongest *recent* velocity in
  2026 (AI prompts +180% YoY, ADHD planners +140%, budget/habit trackers,
  therapy worksheets +75–90%) plus high-volume evergreen one-pagers.
- **Batch 3 (33–64)** — what's trending on TikTok / Reddit / Pinterest / X /
  YouTube right now: faceless-content & social planners, parenting charts,
  faith & prayer pages, fitness/cycle trackers, home organization, wedding
  planning, pet care, and wellness logs.
- **Batch 4 (65–128)** — premium & geo-localized: products aimed at
  higher-income buyers (wealth/investing, executive & business-owner tools,
  affluent lifestyle, affluent parents) and locale-specific tax/bookkeeping
  (UK HMRC, Canada CRA, Australia ATO, US IRS). These command higher price
  points ($5–$40+) and face far less competition than generic planners.
- **Batch 5 (129–160)** — niche-profession one-pagers for high-income
  professionals who already pay for templates: real estate agents, lawyers,
  CPAs/bookkeepers, consultants, financial advisors, clinicians, therapists,
  contractors, architects, photographers, property managers, loan officers,
  insurance agents, event planners, and HR.
- **Batch 6 (161–200)** — the rest of the professions, so the catalog covers
  *every* career: education, skilled trades, food & hospitality, health &
  wellness, creative & performing arts, tech & digital, sales & support,
  transportation & operations, and care & household services.
- **Batch 7 (201–250)** — deep-niche products for **affluent, consistent
  spenders**: collectors & connoisseurs (watches, wine, whiskey, coins, stamps,
  rare books, antiques, jewelry, sneakers, luxury goods, collector cars, art),
  equine & kennel owners, yachting & private aviation, family-office & wealth
  tools (trust, philanthropy, DAF, syndication, angel/VC, 1031), concierge
  health & appearance, and luxury lifestyle services (private chef, estate
  staff, concierge, second homes, private clubs, bespoke, interior & landscape
  design, ski & safari travel, smart homes). These buyers spend thousands per
  year on their hobbies and homes — and pay premium prices for tools that match.

The catalog is a **global store**. Beyond the 200 English originals, the eight
most universal bestsellers (sales tracker, budget planner, habit tracker, meal
planner, password log, packing list, SMART goals, and workout log) are also
produced in six languages — Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, and
Dutch — under `i18n/` (see [International versions](#international-versions)).

Every file is a **single page** with a consistent design system: an accent
header band, clearly separated sections, ruled fill-in fields, checkable boxes,
and a footer. Text is drawn with DejaVu Sans (full Unicode support), so nothing
gets dropped or mangled.

## Regenerating

```bash
pip install fpdf2
python3 1-page-pdfs/generate_pdfs.py        # all 250 English PDFs
python3 1-page-pdfs/generate_i18n.py        # 48 translated PDFs → i18n/
```

The generator (`generate_pdfs.py`) is the single source of truth for the 250
English products — edit the product functions there and re-run to rebuild all
PDFs deterministically. `generate_i18n.py` imports that generator and produces
the translated set from its own `STRINGS` dictionaries.

## Design notes

- US Letter (215.9 × 279.4 mm), 12 mm margins, single column.
- DejaVu Sans regular/bold from `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/`.
- Accent palettes are chosen per category (finance, games, clinical, therapy,
  music, home/farm).
- Every PDF is validated at build time to stay within page bounds on one page.
