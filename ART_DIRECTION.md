# Art direction — The Ritual Library

## Brand premise
A book from The Ritual Library should feel like a quiet object of care before a reader writes a word. The KDP scouts use restrained typography, warm whitespace, simple linework, and one deep cover color per product. Deluxe editions deepen that experience through paper, binding, tactile finish, and packaging—not by pretending a paperback contains those materials.

## Visual system

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Cover | Deep mineral color, one warm accent, concentric-line motif, generous negative space, short cover title. |
| Interior | Black-and-white KDP-safe layouts: high contrast, 6.8 pt+ utility copy, 8.2 pt+ labels, relaxed writing space. |
| Typography | Sans-serif hierarchy: concise labels, calm plain-language prompts, never dense “worksheet” pages. |
| Icons | Simple lines, color-independent text labels, and no critical instruction dependent only on color. |
| Photography/listing assets | Real interior previews first; clean daylight; no body transformation imagery, medical imagery, or implied therapeutic outcome. |

## Collection color direction

| Collection | Cover mood | Accent mood |
|---|---|---|
| Pace & Progress | Midnight navy, moss, plum, warm clay | Eucalyptus, soft gold, peach, pale blue |
| Stillwork Editions | Deep ocean, forest, slate, warm umber | Sea glass, cedar, brass, oat, blush |

## Listing-image rules
1. Use `cover.jpg` only as the Amazon main image after confirming the platform’s current main-image rules.
2. Use the four interior images to make the actual writing experience visible; never replace them with generic lifestyle imagery.
3. `listing_06_callout.jpg` communicates only verifiable paperback facts: trim, pages, interior, and scout price.
4. `listing_07_series.jpg` supports collection discovery; it does not promise that other products are included.
5. Any image mentioning a digital audio companion, card deck, foil, ribbon, or box must be tied to the exact physical SKU that contains it.

## Deluxe direction
- **Dose & Breathe:** cloth/bookcloth case, foil + blind deboss, sewn signatures, warm 100 gsm paper, two ribbons, elastic, rear pocket.
- **Pocket of Calm:** matte durable cards, color + text family navigation, 100 gsm logbook, rigid box. Exact box, magnetic closure, and card finish are selected only after a vendor quote/proof.

## Accessibility
- Maintain high contrast for text on all covers and listing assets.
- Repeat color categories with written family names or icons.
- Keep prompts optional; do not require breath holds, numerical tracking, or disclosure of private health information.
- Supply captions/transcripts for any eventual audio/video bonus.
