# Raising the Catalog: A Book-by-Book Quality Dissertation

Date: 2026-08-28. Branch: ADHD-Journals (ours). Status: COMPLETE. All 18 of our books treated in one pass, worst first.

## Abstract

Each of our 18 titles was given a direct review of its actual files, niche research, a diagnosis on a five-dimension scale, and only then actions. Owner rules for this work: listing files and covers may change, interiors may not; the other agent's 36-book kit line is read-only and receives advice only.

Final result: catalog total 655 to 735 points, average 36.4 to 40.8, every gain landing on listing and cover work with all 18 interior PDFs verified byte-identical. Both validators PERFECT after every step; the catalog keyword guard held 18 titles x 7 keywords, all unique.

## Method

Five dimensions, 10 points each: D1 interior depth (locked by owner rule), D2 niche demand (partly movable by shelf placement), D3 differentiation, D4 cover quality (measured, not judged: luminance, contrast, saturation against our own catalog norm of contrast 14 to 16), D5 listing health (keywords, counts, sync, claims).

Honesty rules: every design count stated on a listing was counted from the actual PDF art pages: garden 47, firststroke 37, mosaic 57, woodland 57, fractal 67, architect 67, botanical 49, celestial 49, cozy 49, tidal 49. No medical claims, no community or brand names we do not own, nothing the interior cannot back up.

Gates after every book: gen_catalog keyword guard, validate_nine.py and validate_batch4.py both PERFECT, interior hash unchanged, wrap spine recomputed from the same page count.

## Systemic defects found and fixed catalog-wide

1. Design counts were stated nowhere; every competitor on every shelf we researched leads with them.
2. Printed cover subtitles disagreed with listing subtitles on 15 of 18 books (KDP wants them in sync). All synced by regenerating covers.
3. Two cover badges said AMATEUR where they meant INTERMEDIATE (mosaic, woodland).
4. Descriptions duplicated the ladder guidance (DESC_APPEND plus the line gen_catalog already appends).
5. Three titles lacked the "for Adults" search phrase; two flagship titles (5-Minute Dump, Dopamine Menu) carried no descriptor at all.
6. Eight covers measured below catalog norm for contrast, mostly pale grounds; all were moved onto deeper grounds.
