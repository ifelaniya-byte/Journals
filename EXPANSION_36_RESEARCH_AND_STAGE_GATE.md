## 3. Product architecture: unique by design

The 36 candidates are intentionally not a cover-swap catalog. Each requires all of the following before it can move from candidate to a production brief:

- a different primary customer job and a written differentiation hypothesis;
- original prompt, activity, visual, page-flow, or object architecture—not merely a changed cover, audience label, or keyword set;
- a unique editorial/visual concept, cover, title/subtitle, metadata, product images, and evidence log;
- a right-sized format: paperback only where a bound book is genuinely useful; actual card, kit, folio, desk-pad, keepsake, or print-at-home test where that better matches the job;
- product-specific review for food, plants, travel, tools, music/copyright, privacy, events, safety, and accessibility boundaries; and
- current platform/printer review before a format/channel/identifier decision.

### Guardrails built into the 36 concepts

- Food candidates record personal recipes or hosting memories; they do not provide nutrition, dietary, allergen, food-safety, or alcohol-service advice.
- Garden/nature candidates use observation and craft prompts; they do not provide edible-plant, pesticide, allergy, wildlife, or safety advice.
- Repair candidates are decision/log tools; they do not provide electrical, plumbing, structural, warranty, or tool instructions.
- Music candidates prohibit lyrics, album art, sheet music, and other unlicensed third-party assets.
- Moving/home candidates do not give legal, real-estate, landlord, security, or contractor advice.
- Work candidates do not make performance, employment, accommodation, legal, or business-results promises.
- No candidate is a therapy, health, financial, diagnostic, treatment, caregiving, or relationship-counseling product.

## 4. Staged path toward an actual production package

The owner selected “full production” as the eventual objective. That objective cannot be truthfully achieved by generating 36 books in a batch. The controlled path is:

### Stage 0 — portfolio and rights gate

1. Keep E01–E36 outside the canonical 18-SKU portfolio until a named human approves a candidate after evidence.
2. Complete the existing identity/counsel gate; do not place an unapproved imprint, author line, or domain in any candidate asset.
3. Complete a title/keyword/common-law screen and counsel route for any candidate selected for production.
4. Record ownership, licenses, contributor terms, and original-asset provenance before production.

### Stage 1 — validate six distinct customer jobs

1. A named human selects no more than the six priority pilots above.
2. Build one small, product-specific, low-data test artifact per pilot, such as a sample spread, physical dummy, or print-at-home page set.
3. Ask a defined audience about usefulness, clarity, format preference, and willingness to buy; do not use health/financial/other sensitive profiling or collect unnecessary personal data.
4. Preserve raw evidence, dates, sample version, recruitment method, feedback, and exclusions. Do not treat likes, AI scores, or an undated trend article as sales validation.
5. Retire, revise, or advance each pilot by a pre-committed human decision—not a batch quota.

### Stage 2 — six independent product briefs

Only for a pilot with documented human approval, prepare a separate brief covering: audience/job, competitive differentiation, page architecture, final product form, source and asset rights, visual direction, accessibility, claims boundary, production/print route, potential channels, verification plan, and proof criteria. A separate bilingual edition is out of scope until an English edition individually clears its complete route.

### Stage 3 — a single final product at a time

For each approved brief: author original material → design the unique interior/cover → independent editorial/content review → qualified specialty review where required → build final files → current printer/platform validation → physical proof → price/channel decision → named human release. This sequence is the minimum path; it is not an upload authorization.

## 5. Explicit non-results

The owner-directed local build created 36 controlled original prototype packages, but it does **not** create 36 cleared commercial products, translate any product, add any SKU to the existing portfolio, set prices, make listings, acquire identifiers, gather customer data, contact suppliers, establish accounts, order manufacture, or perform any public or paid action. The existing 18 products retain their current gates and statuses unchanged.
