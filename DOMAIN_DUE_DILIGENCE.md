## What this changes

1. The previously selected defensive-registration scope—`calyven.com` plus `calyvenstudio.com` for one year—**cannot be completed as specified**. This is not a payment or access issue: `calyven.com` is already registered and in active use.
2. Do not pay for `calyvenstudio.com` as a workaround. It would not secure the root the business actually needs and could create a closer-confusion question.
3. Keep all current catalog identity fields private and unchanged. The current `Stillwork Studio` configuration is not promoted by this result; it remains an uncleared legacy candidate.
4. Counsel should search Calyven alongside the other terms only to give a documented no-go/qualification opinion, then propose/clear a replacement root before any defensive registration.

## Purchase control once a replacement is selected

For a term counsel approves for defensive registration, buy the exact-root `.com` and the exact presentation `.com` (for example, `<root>.com` and `<root>studio.com`) only after a current registrar checkout confirms availability. Use a one-year term, registrant privacy if lawful/available, auto-renew, two-factor authentication, registrar lock, and no DNS/web deployment. The purchase must be made by the owner through an authenticated registrar/payment account; this workspace cannot complete a paid domain transaction.
