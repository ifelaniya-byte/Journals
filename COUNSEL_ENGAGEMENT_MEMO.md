# Counsel engagement brief — identity, claims, and release boundaries

**Prepared:** August 28, 2026
**Decision owner:** Founder / brand lead
**Requested scope:** one coordinated U.S. trademark/name and healthcare-marketing/claims review.
**Business state:** all materials are private prepublication candidates. This brief does not authorize KDP publication, paid advertising, manufacturing, public domain use, or a legal conclusion from anyone other than retained counsel.

## Executive request

Please provide one scoped written assessment before the business commits to a public imprint, domain, QR proof, KDP upload, paid campaign, or production order.

1. **Identity / trademark package:** Assess the four active identity terms—**Stillwork Studio**, **The Ritual Library**, **Pace & Progress**, and **Quiet Practice**—plus the six Wave 1 product names, in the proposed U.S. printed-goods, retail, and optional digital-audio context. These are listed in **counsel-review priority**, not as a claim that any is available or should be adopted.
2. **Claims / marketing:** Identify permitted, prohibited, qualified, or substantiation-dependent language for the six Wave 1 paperbacks. Review titles, keywords, metadata, covers, listing images, customer-service copy, creator materials, QR pages/transcripts/audio, and channel-specific paid-media approaches.
3. **Fallback sequencing:** State whether any active term is clear/revise/do-not-use, whether coexistence steps are needed, and recommend a replacement-imprint screening path if Stillwork is not usable. Do not rely on the rejected candidates in Appendix A as substitute marks without a fresh search.

### Requested search parameters

For each term, search exact, phonetic, visual, commercial-impression, foreign-equivalent where relevant, federal/state/common-law, marketplace, ISBN/imprint, domain, social-handle, and related-goods uses. Cover at least:

- **Class 16:** printed books, journals, planners, cards, calendars, and stationery;
- **Class 35:** online and wholesale retail; and
- **Class 41:** online non-downloadable audio/content.

Please advise whether a downloadable/digital class or another class is appropriate. A Git tag, private file, generated metadata field, or unpublished candidate page is not evidence of clearance or marketplace use.

Review each final package as a personal-reflection product—not a medical device, therapy, treatment, financial service, or deluxe object.
