## Executive request

Please provide a scoped written assessment that answers the two questions below before the business commits to a public imprint, branded domain, QR proof, KDP upload, or paid campaign.

1. **Name / trademark:** May the business use and, if appropriate, pursue protection for the coordinated candidate package **Calyven Studio** (imprint), **The Ritual Library** (editorial brand), **Pace & Progress** (Collection A), and **Quiet Practice** (Collection B), plus the six Wave 1 product names, in the proposed U.S. paper-goods, retail, and digital-audio context? 2. **Claims / marketing:** Which claims, disclaimers, titles, keywords, creator statements, QR/audio scripts, landing-page wording, and paid-media approaches are permissible for the six Wave 1 paperbacks—especially A01 *Dose & Breathe*—and which require revision, substantiation, exclusion, age gating, or no paid use?

### B. Healthcare-marketing, claims, and advertising

Please review each Wave 1 package, its final KDP metadata, cover/wrap, listing images, A+ draft, customer-service copy, creator brief, optional QR landing page, transcript, and audio script. The product is a personal-reflection paperback—not a medical device, therapy, treatment, financial service, or deluxe object.

| SKU | Product | Review priority / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A01 | *Dose & Breathe* | GLP-1/medication and care-team context. Confirm whether title/subtitle/keyword/language is acceptable; no dosing, injection, safety, symptom, or outcome advice. No named prescription-drug marks in public metadata/ads unless specifically approved. |
| A04 | *Softer Words* | Personal reflection only; no psychotherapy, trauma, depression, or anxiety-treatment implication. |
| A05 | *Night Harbor* | Wind-down reflection only; no insomnia/sleep-treatment or medication/supplement implication. |
| B10 | *Rest & Regulate* | Breath-paced, optional reflection only; no vagus, HRV, nervous-system, anxiety-treatment, or required breath-pattern claim. |
| B12 | *Back to Enough* | Planning support only; no burnout diagnosis, recovery, employment, or mental-health treatment claim. |
| B18 | *Enough Money, Enough Calm* | Reflection only; no financial, legal, tax, investment, or mental-health advice/outcome claim. |

**Platform boundary:** paid Meta, TikTok, and Amazon Ads for Collection A remain off until platform/account/creative-specific review is documented. Counsel should identify any additional prohibition or required qualifier for creator seeding, organic social, email, retail descriptions, or QR landing pages. No campaign may target, assert, solicit, or profile a person’s medical condition or prescription use.

**Requested output:** a marked copy review, approved/prohibited phrase list, required disclaimer language and placement, platform/channel limitations, and review conditions for subsequent copy changes.
