DESCRIPTION:
One page a day, done slowly. The Slow Page asks for less than any journal you've abandoned: one thing done slowly, four sensory details from the day, a list of what you're deliberately not doing, and a checkbox that says tonight, this was enough. Four season gates — spring, summer, autumn, winter — each with intentions and a hand-drawn emblem, plus weekly sabbath pages for one walk, one unhurried meal, and one hour off screens. 144 undated pages, 6 × 9 in, cream paper. For anyone recovering from hurry.
