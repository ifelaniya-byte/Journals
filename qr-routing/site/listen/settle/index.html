Transcript

Find a position that feels workable for you. You may notice the room, the support beneath you, or one ordinary sound. If a natural breath feels comfortable, let it come and go without trying to change it. If it does not, simply keep reading or return to the page. This practice is optional, adaptable, and yours to leave at any time.
