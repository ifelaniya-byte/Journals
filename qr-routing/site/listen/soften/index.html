Softer Words

This optional audio pause accompanies the paperback. Use it only if it feels useful; it does not replace professional care or advice.

Transcript

Take a moment before you begin. You do not have to make today sound better than it was. Notice one thing that felt hard, one thing that helped, or one sentence you would offer someone you care about. The page is not asking for a perfect answer. A few honest words, or a blank space, can be enough.
