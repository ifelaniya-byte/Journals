Enough Money, Enough Calm

This optional audio pause accompanies the paperback. Use it only if it feels useful; it does not replace professional care or advice.

Transcript

Before making a money decision, make room for one small pause. You might write what you know, what you are feeling, and what question still needs a qualified answer. This page does not need you to solve everything today. Choose one manageable next step, or simply give yourself time before reacting.
