## Safest pilot

Begin with six products representing distinct buyer needs:

1. Bill Day
2. Care Transition Home
3. Home Inventory Atlas
4. College Application Map
5. Workplace Accommodation Paper Trail
6. Invoice Follow-Up

These provide a balanced test of money, caregiving, property, education, accessibility/work, and small-business use cases.

## Human release checklist

For each pilot product, the account owner must personally confirm:

- Final title, subtitle, description, categories, and keywords
- Interior and cover proof are readable and aligned
- Product contains no professional advice or guaranteed outcome
- Rights, imprint, and publisher information are correct
- Price and distribution settings are correct
- Expanded Distribution remains OFF unless separately approved
- Proof copy or digital proof has been inspected
- Publication is approved for that specific title

## Marketplace sequence

1. Complete publisher identity, tax, and payment verification personally.
2. Upload six pilot products manually.
3. Inspect digital previews and order proof copies where available.
4. Publish only the titles individually approved by the owner.
5. Wait for rejection, quality, and customer signals.
6. Revise metadata or interiors before expanding the catalog.

## Non-live advertising preparation

Approved positioning language:

- “Practical pages for keeping the details of a recurring life task in one place.”
- “A structured, undated record system for questions, documents, follow-ups, and next steps.”
- “Designed for clarity and organization—not professional advice.”

Do not use claims of medical improvement, financial savings, legal outcomes, emergency guarantees, or guaranteed productivity. Do not launch paid ads until the owner sets a budget and the corresponding listing is live.

The repository is the source of truth. A preview-only catalog can be generated from `CATALOG-MASTER`; no checkout, payment, customer-data collection, or public marketplace publishing is included in this package.
