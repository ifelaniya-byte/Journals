# Human-Style Catalog Review

**Review date:** 2026-08-31  
**Reviewer mode:** human-gate quality review performed by Arena agent  
**Scope:** Products 1–288 plus Deep Need 36  
**Decision:** HOLD — do not publish yet

## Executive decision

The repository is complete and synchronized, but the catalog is **not a 100% publish-ready catalog**. Asset presence and PDF readability pass. The later generated batches (109–288 and the Deep Need 36 slate) need editorial and visual refinement before marketplace publication.

## Passes

- Git branch is synchronized with origin.
- Original 1–36 remain present and frozen.
- Products 37–288 are present in seven 36-product volumes.
- Deep Need 36 is present as a separate candidate slate.
- Required kit files are present for the volume products.
- Deep Need print and digital PDFs match byte-for-byte.
- PDF interiors open successfully and meet their declared page counts.
- Candidate-only status and human-review hold are stated in listing copy.
- No marketplace upload, publication, advertising, or account action was performed.

## Holds requiring correction

### 1. Interior differentiation — HIGH

Volumes 5–9 use a highly repetitive generic prompt-page architecture. Product names differ, but many interiors do not yet provide a genuinely distinct mechanic tailored to the stated niche. This is a meaningful low-content and customer-value risk.

**Required correction:** give each title a demonstrably specific page system: fields, workflows, examples, review pages, and terminology appropriate to its actual use case.

### 2. Deep Need cover quality — HIGH

The Deep Need JPG covers are production placeholders rather than finished branded covers. They do not contain readable title typography or a developed visual system.

**Required correction:** replace with final branded covers, verify dimensions, bleed, spine calculations, and title legibility at thumbnail size.

### 3. Deep Need interior differentiation — HIGH

The Deep Need interiors share the same repeated generic prompts across all 36 titles. The validation report verifies files and page counts, but that is not the same as verifying meaningful product differentiation.

**Required correction:** build title-specific forms and review pages, then rerun content and PDF validation.

### 4. Research traceability — MEDIUM

The research brief identifies authoritative source families, but the product-level mapping from each source to each title is not sufficiently explicit.

**Required correction:** add a source-and-rationale row for every title, with access date and the specific user problem supported. Avoid copying protected text.

### 5. Marketplace metadata — MEDIUM

The generated listings are intentionally conservative but minimal. They need title/subtitle polish, accurate categories, seven non-repeating keywords, audience clarity, and product-specific descriptions.

**Required correction:** complete an editorial metadata pass and rerun cross-catalog keyword checks.

### 6. Sensitive-topic review — MEDIUM

Financial, tax, legal, benefits, emergency, medical, custody-adjacent, and accessibility products must receive a manual claims review. They should remain organizational tools and must not imply professional advice, eligibility, guaranteed results, or legal conclusions.

## Release recommendation

- **Original 1–36:** retain frozen candidate status; publish only after the existing human gate.
- **Products 37–108:** retain current verification status; perform normal final editorial review.
- **Products 109–288:** HOLD pending differentiated-interior and metadata work.
- **Deep Need 36:** HOLD pending finished covers, differentiated interiors, source mapping, and final metadata review.
