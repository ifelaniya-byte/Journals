# Cashflow — Range Band Press on Amazon

**Live inside the range.** GLP-1 stays on the **cover and title**. The algorithm is fed in the **seven backend keywords**, the **first sentence** of the description, **series**, **$9.99** (60% royalty floor), and **also-bought paths**.

This is not magic. Rank = impressions × CTR × conversion × (later) reviews. We built each of those.

## The loop

```
search  →  thumbnail  →  $9.99 click  →  Look Inside / bullets  →  buy
                                                              ↓
                                                    also-bought trains Wave 2
```

1. **Search.** Backend keywords are the queries (see `listing.txt`). Title is GLP-1 + mechanism so the brand filter does not eat the listing. Generic INN (`semaglutide`) is in keywords, not the title.
2. **CTR.** Discreet covers, $9.99, undated. Do not ads-blast 36 — ads only on Wave 1 until reviews exist.
3. **Conversion.** HTML description: hook (mechanism) → inside → also-in-catalog → $9.99. Proof three trims (01 6×9, 07 5×8, 09 8.5×11) before ads.
4. **Also-bought.** Every listing names 2–3 siblings. Same imprint (**Range Band Press**). Four series on KDP:
   - `GLP-1 Tracking Series` (01–09)
   - `GLP-1 Companion Series` (19–27)
   - `Wellness Tracking Series` (10–18)
   - `Wellness Companion Series` (28–36)
   Create those four series. Do not dump coloring books into them.

## Unit economics (keep $9.99)

US paperback: **$9.99 unlocks 60%**. Below that is 50%. Print ≈ `$0.85 + $0.012 × pages`. Every title in this kit still prints a positive royalty at $9.99.
