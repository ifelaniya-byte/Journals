COPD Breath Scale
Breathlessness Ladder, Inhaler Use As Directed & Air Tick (Undated)

Live inside the range. 'I was short of breath' is not a measurement. COPD Breath Scale scores it on a 0-10 ladder, records inhalers as directed, and ticks what the air was doing that day.

From Range Band Press. A range is not a cage. It is the band you already chose. Undated logs for a life that has numbers but is not a number. Fill in what your own clinician already directed. This book does not diagnose, dose, or treat, and it is not affiliated with any medication manufacturer.

INSIDE
  • Breathlessness ladder — morning, midday, night
  • Inhaler record as directed only
  • Air tick: humidity, smoke, pollen, season
  • Weekly breath pattern for the clinic
