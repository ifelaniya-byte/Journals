Hypermobility Joint Guard
Joint-By-Joint Wear Log, Flare Shield & Safe-Movement Notes (Undated)

Live inside the range. Bendy bodies need a meter, not a warning. Hypermobility Joint Guard logs wear per joint, measures load against answer on a flare shield, and keeps your therapist's notes in one place.

From Range Band Press. A range is not a cage. It is the band you already chose. Undated logs for a life that has numbers but is not a number. Fill in what your own clinician already directed. This book does not diagnose, dose, or treat, and it is not affiliated with any medication manufacturer.
