# $0-to-list census — Range Band Press

Verified **28 August 2026**. Not “the entire internet” — that is not a thing you can scrape. This is every **sell-our-36-title catalog** channel we could verify that is either free to list or takes a **percentage only when a copy sells**. Ranked **least reach → most reach**.

Our files today: **print PDF interiors + wrap covers**, and a **print-at-home PDF**. Not EPUB. Not audiobook. Not inventory.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GO** | $0 before a sale. Accepts our file. 36 titles fit. Upload. |
| **SAME PACK** | GO, but use an existing folder — do not make a new price. |
| **PARK** | $0-ish, but we lack EPUB or a Bowker ISBN, or a hard cap. |
| **NO** | Pay to list, monthly fee, vanity package, or needs stock on a shelf. |

KDP stays **$9.99**. B&N print stays **$14.99**. Lulu Global is **per title, 2× print**. Digital PDFs stay **$9.99**. Do not put $14.99 on Amazon.

## GO now — least popular → most popular

### 1. The Book Patch bookstore — **GO** (tiny)

[thebookpatch.com](https://www.thebookpatch.com) · print + PDF ebook on *their* store

- $0 setup. They take **$0** of print profit (handling $1.50 first copy + $0.10 after + card %). Ebook: they take **$1** per sale.
- No bookstore distribution. Almost no traffic. You send the link.
- Print cost is theirs; $9.99 may not clear. Confirm calculator. If it does not clear, skip the title — do not invent a new catalog price.

### 2. Pothi.com (India) — **GO** (tiny, India)

[pothi.com](https://pothi.com) · India POD

- $0 if you upload ready files. They print when someone orders.
- Audience is India. Trim/paper may not match KDP 6×9 exactly — proof one title (01) before the rest.
- Do not pay for their “publishing packages.”

### 3. DriveThruFiction — **GO** (tiny, PDF-native)

[drivethrufiction.com](https://www.drivethrufiction.com) · OneBookShelf / Roll20 family

- $0 publisher account. Built for **PDF**. POD exists via Lightning Source with **no Ingram setup fee**.
- Audience is RPG/fiction hobbyists, not GLP-1 search. Still a free shelf.
- Upload `DIGITAL-9.99` PDFs at $9.99. Skip their print until you confirm ISBN rules on a dummy title.

### 4. itch.io — **GO / SAME PACK** (small)

[itch.io](https://itch.io) · classification: **book** · app: itch.io desktop

- $0 to list. Open revenue share (you pick; default **10%**). Stripe/PayPal on top.
- New accounts: **soft cap ~20 project pages**. We have 36. Upload Wave 1 first, then [ask support](https://itch.io/support) to lift the cap. Do not pay.
- File: `MARKETS/DIGITAL-9.99/` PDF + JPG. Price $9.99.

### 5. Ko-fi Shop — **GO / SAME PACK** (small)

[ko-fi.com](https://ko-fi.com) · app: Ko-fi

- $0/month. Shop sales **5%** + Stripe/PayPal. Tips 0% on Free. Do **not** buy Gold ($12/mo).
- No search traffic. Link-in-bio store. Same PDF pack, $9.99.

### 6. Buy Me a Coffee shop — **GO / SAME PACK** (small)

[buymeacoffee.com](https://www.buymeacoffee.com)

- $0/month. Flat **5%** + processing. Same PDF pack, $9.99.
- Pick **either** Ko-fi **or** BMC, not both, unless you like double uploads. Ko-fi is enough.

### 7. Whop — **GO / SAME PACK** (small–mid)

[whop.com](https://whop.com)

- $0/month. Direct sales ~**2.7–3%** + card. Marketplace listing $0 (old 30% Discover cut is dead as of 2025).
- More “course/community” than journals. Use if you want a storefront with Discord later. Same PDF pack.

### 8. Lemon Squeezy — **GO / SAME PACK** (small–mid)

[lemonsqueezy.com](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com) · Stripe-owned MoR

- $0/month. **5% + $0.50** per sale (tax handled). No listing fee.
- Good if EU VAT scares you. Same PDF pack, $9.99.

### 9. Square Online (free plan) — **GO** (own store, no traffic)

[squareup.com](https://squareup.com)

- $0/month. Unlimited products. Online cut ~**3.3% + $0.30**.
- You must send traffic. Square branding on the free plan. Fine as a backup checkout, not a market.

### 10. Systeme.io free / Kit Commerce — **GO** (own store, no traffic)

- Systeme.io: $0, ~3 funnels, 0% platform (processor still takes a cut).
- Kit (ConvertKit) Commerce: $0 to 10k subs, **3.5% + $0.30**.
- Only if you already have an email list. We do not. Skip until Wave 1 has buyers.

### 11. Payhip Free — **GO / SAME PACK** (mid)

[payhip.com](https://payhip.com) · pack `MARKETS/DIGITAL-9.99/`

- $0/month. **5%** + Stripe/PayPal. EU VAT handled.
- Do not upgrade to $29/$99. Cheapest serious PDF checkout after processing.

### 12. Gumroad — **GO / SAME PACK** (mid)

[gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com) · pack `MARKETS/DIGITAL-9.99/` · app: Gumroad

- $0/month. Direct **10% + $0.50** (processing often bundled in that number depending on plan copy — confirm at checkout). Discover feed is **30%** — leave Discover **off**.
- Modest discovery. Same PDF pack, $9.99.

### 13. Lulu Bookstore (direct) — **GO** (mid, print + ebook)

[lulu.com](https://www.lulu.com)

- Print on Lulu’s own store: **$9.99**, same KDP PDFs. $0 setup.
- Ebook on Lulu’s own store: PDF/EPUB, they keep ~10%, **no $4.99 fee**.
- **Do not** turn on Lulu *ebook* Global Dist ($4.99/title). Print Global Dist is the other Lulu door (next).

### 14. Lulu print Global Distribution — **GO** (mid)

Pack `MARKETS/Lulu-Global/`

- $0. List ≥ **2× print cost**, x.99, never under $9.99. Title **30** is **$11.99**; the other 35 estimate **$9.99**. Confirm in their calculator.
- Extra Ingram-fed reach. Not a substitute for B&N Press.

### 15. Google Play Books — **GO** (large digital)

[play.google.com/books/publish](https://play.google.com/books/publish) · pack `MARKETS/DIGITAL-9.99/`

- $0. ~**70%** of list. PDF **or** EPUB. Free Google Book ID (no ISBN).
- **App:** Google Play Books (Android, web, iOS reader).
- Description must say **print-at-home PDF of the paperback**.
- Price band about $0.05–$200. We stay $9.99.

### 16. Barnes & Noble Press **print** — **GO** (large US print)

[press.barnesandnoble.com](https://press.barnesandnoble.com) · pack `MARKETS/BN-Press/`

- $0. Free B&N ISBN. Catalog floor **$14.99** every title (22 Apr 2026).
- ~55% of list − print. Cap ~100 live titles; we have 36.
- **Site:** BN.com. **App:** Nook / B&N.
- Never upload this $14.99 listing to KDP.
- Nook *ebook* is a different file (EPUB) — parked below.

### 17. Amazon KDP **print** — **GO** (largest)

[kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com) · pack `KDP-Complete-Kit/` · **$9.99**

- $0. 60% − print on Amazon.com. Expanded Distribution ON (40% − print, $0 extra).
- **Sites:** Amazon.com and KDP’s other storefronts (match 60% floors: usually UK £7.99, euro €9.99).
- **App:** Amazon shopping app. Kindle app is *ebook* — parked below.
- Do **not** enroll KDP Select if Google Play PDFs are live.

That is the whole **GO** list that matches “no money until a sale” **and** a file we actually have.
