Your brain runs on stimulation. The Dopamine Menu turns regulating it into a restaurant you run. Build your menu in five courses — starters under five minutes, mains for deep flow, sides for the ten-minute gaps, rationed specials, and portioned desserts — then order from it daily. Each order logs your appetite, what you ordered, and the after-taste rating, so you learn what actually feeds your brain versus what steals from it. Menu refreshes keep the offerings honest, and a two-page index re-copies your working menu. 150 undated pages, 6 × 9 in, cream paper. For ADHD, AuDHD, and anyone whose stimulation diet needs a chef. Mechanism: five courses (starters, mains, sides, specials, desserts) plus a daily order ticket.
