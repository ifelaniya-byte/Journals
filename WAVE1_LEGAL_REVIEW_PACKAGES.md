## A01: Dose & Breathe — Legal Review Package

### Product Summary
- **Title:** Dose & Breathe: A Mindful Weekly GLP-1 Companion
- **Format:** KDP Paperback | 6×8 | 128 pages
- **List Price:** $16.99
- **Audience:** GLP-1 medication users (semaglutide, tirzepatide, etc.)
- **Positioning:** Weekly reflection/journaling companion for GLP-1 users

### Review Scope: GLP-1-Specific Claims

**Please review for:**

1. **GLP-1 Positioning Language**
   - Does the product correctly position as "companion" and not "medical device," "treatment," or "therapeutic intervention"?
   - Are there any implied claims about medication efficacy, dosing, or medical benefits?
   - Any language suggesting this product prevents or treats side effects?

2. **Dosing/Injection Language** (Red Flags)
   - Are there any instructions for "correct dosing" or "safe injection"?
   - Any language about medication timing or adjustment?
   - Any implied advice about when/how to use GLP-1?

3. **Health Outcome Claims** (Red Flags)
   - Any weight-loss claims or "lose weight" language?
   - Any blood-sugar, glucose, or metabolic outcome claims?
   - Any side-effect reduction or prevention claims?

4. **Content Accuracy**
   - Are the weekly breath visualizations described as optional?
   - Are care-team notes framed as questions, not medical advice?
   - Is it clear a medical provider's guidance takes precedence?

5. **Third-Party Trademark**
   - Are any GLP-1 drug names (semaglutide, tirzepatide, Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro) used without permission?
   - Are generic references sufficient and accurate?
