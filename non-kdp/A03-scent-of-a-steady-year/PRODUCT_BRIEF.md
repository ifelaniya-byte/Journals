## Actual product

A **10 × 12 in. wire-bound wall calendar with a reinforced hanger**, designed as an undated planning and sensory-cue ritual object. It has twelve heavy matte monthly sheets and a storage envelope for its sticker set.

The core customer job is a useful year-at-a-glance object that turns routine days into non-medical sensory cues for care. Its standard version uses visual/scent-themed cues only. A separate scented-sticker edition would require material, skin-contact, packaging, marketplace, and claims review before any test.
