# Wave 1 human content-QA protocol

**Purpose:** The structural validator proves that files exist and fit. This protocol proves that a human reader can use the book, understands what it is, does not encounter a harmful claim, and receives the quiet, non-judgmental tone promised by The Ritual Library.

**Applies only to the six Wave 1 scouts:** A01 *Dose & Breathe*, A04 *Softer Words*, A05 *Night Harbor*, B10 *Rest & Regulate*, B12 *Back to Enough*, and B18 *Enough Money, Enough Calm*.

**Timing:** Complete before KDP upload. Review the exported PDF **and** again from the KDP Previewer/proof before ads.

**Independence:** The creator should not be the only reader. Use one editorial reviewer and one representative target reader where feasible. The legal/claims reviewer remains a separate gate.

---

## 1. Review standard for every page

Review every actual page—not merely sample spreads. Initial each row on the relevant product log or record page range and exceptions in the Scorecard Gate Log.

### Every-page checks
- [ ] Title, prompt, label, line, checkbox, and page number are readable at normal print size.
- [ ] Page has no clipped text, accidental overlap, orphaned heading, broken line, illegible contrast, inconsistent margin, or unwanted blank/duplicate content.
- [ ] Prompt is specific enough to use, optional in tone, and free of shame, scarcity, body judgment, performance pressure, or a requirement to disclose private health information.
- [ ] Tone supports **today / experiment / support / notice / choose / enough**; it does not use **cheat / fix / failure / cure / should / must** except where required in a disclaimer.
- [ ] No page introduces a diagnosis, outcome claim, protocol, dosing instruction, health guarantee, treatment implication, financial recommendation, or unsupported safety statement.
- [ ] No page depicts or promises deluxe materials not in the paperback: cards, foil, ribbons, pocket, box, scent, QR audio, magnetic closure, or specialty paper.
- [ ] If a prompt may be skipped or adapted, that permission is plainly available in nearby context.

### Cover/listing truthfulness checks
- [ ] Cover title and subtitle match `metadata.txt` without misleading material or outcome language.
- [ ] The short cover title is elegant; the searchable Amazon title is used only in KDP metadata/listing fields.
- [ ] Interior-preview images are actual pages from the book, not a prettier unshippable prototype.
- [ ] `listing_06_callout.jpg` accurately states page count, trim, black-and-white/white-paper interior, and scout price.
- [ ] `listing_07_series.jpg` presents a collection relationship only; it does not say a buyer receives other books.

### Stop-the-line issues
Any one of these blocks upload until corrected: a medical/treatment/dosing/financial claim; a third-party mark used without clearance; a false format/material implication; unreadable copy; a misleading KDP title/subtitle; a page with production defect; placeholder author/domain text; or an inaccessible critical instruction.

## 2. Product review maps

Page ranges reflect the current generated files. If a rebuild changes a page count, regenerate this map from the content structure before sign-off.

### A01 — *Dose & Breathe* (128 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | GLP-1 wording, title, subtitle, categories, keywords | Does the package say “companion/reflection,” never dose instruction, medication advice, or outcome promise? |
| 1–2 | Title + note before beginning | Is the disclaimer complete but calm, and is the brand/author placeholder resolved before upload? |
| 3–8 | Welcome, support map, use guidance, personal compass | Does every page make clear that care-team guidance takes precedence and routine fields are optional? |
| 9–62 | 24 weekly two-page rituals + six monthly reflection pages | Check every weekly label and breath visual. Is breathing optional and described without physiological claim? Are care-team notes framed as questions/observations, not advice? |
| 63–82 | Gentle-return pages | Are all prompts supportive and non-weight-centric? |
| 83–128 | Open notes | Check lines, footer, page count, and no accidental repeated heading/layout fault. |

**Specific red flags:** “dose correctly,” “reduce side effects,” “safe after injection,” weight-loss guarantee, injection instructions, named drug trademark, or a pulse/symptom recommendation.

### A04 — *Softer Words* (128 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | Gift positioning, self-talk language, categories, keywords | Does it read as a diary/reflection practice—not therapy or mood treatment? |
| 1–4 | Title, note, welcome, believable-affirmation entry | Are affirmations plausible and invitational rather than falsely positive? |
| 5–106 | 90 daily entries + 12 weekly evidence-of-care pages | Read every prompt. Does the daily rhythm leave room for difficulty instead of demanding gratitude? |
| 107–112 | Six monthly letters/reflections | Does “monthly” remain undated and flexible? |
| 113–128 | Free-write pages | Check writing space, lines, page numbers, and bleed-free layout. |

**Specific red flags:** “cure,” “heal trauma,” “eliminate anxiety/depression,” forced positivity, spiritual promises, or guilt around missed entries.

### A05 — *Night Harbor* (168 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | Sleep-adjacent title/subtitle/keywords | Does it promise a wind-down reflection—not sleep treatment, insomnia relief, or a medical result? |
| 1–4 | Title, note, welcome, personal wind-down menu | Is the language gentle enough for difficult nights and clear that the reader can stop/seek help? |
| 5–100 | 84 nightly entries + 12 weekly morning-light pages | Check every prompt. Does every sleep/comfort field allow an observation without a score or promise? |
| 101–168 | Night notes | Check page flow, interior margins, and that no notes page repeats a clinical claim. |

**Specific red flags:** “fall asleep faster,” “treat insomnia,” sleep-audio claim, medication/supplement suggestion, or a promise that the book causes restorative sleep.

### B10 — *Rest & Regulate* (144 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | Breathwork/nervous-system wording | Does it stay at personal grounding/reflection and avoid vagus, HRV, or treatment promises? |
| 1–4 | Title, note, welcome, practice menu | Is every breath practice optional, natural, and clearly adaptable? Is there no required breath hold? |
| 5–106 | 90 daily practices + 12 weekly reflections | Read every practice and prompt. Could a reader with panic/breath discomfort choose an alternative with no penalty? |
| 107–144 | Practice notes | Verify the same tone and physical page quality. |

**Specific red flags:** “regulates your nervous system,” “stimulates vagus nerve,” “improves HRV,” “reduces anxiety,” forced breath pattern, or claim of physiological effect.

### B12 — *Back to Enough* (160 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | Burnout/productivity wording | Does it offer planning support without diagnosing burnout or promising recovery/productivity results? |
| 1–5 | Title, note, welcome, early signs, four moves | Do “delay/delegate/delete/do imperfectly” cues feel permission-giving rather than prescriptive? |
| 6–101 | 84 daily rescue pages + 12 weekly resets | Check every task/triage prompt. Does it respect low capacity and avoid moralizing unfinished work? |
| 102–113 | 12 mini-break plan pages | Are pauses optional, safe, and compatible with varied workplaces/access needs? |
| 114–160 | Working notes | Check all pages for consistent labels and usable writing space. |

**Specific red flags:** “treat burnout,” “prevent depression,” employment/legal advice, guaranteed productivity, pressure to disclose private workplace information.

### B18 — *Enough Money, Enough Calm* (168 pp.)

| Pages | What reviewer reads/checks | Product-specific approval question |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + metadata | Money/financial-anxiety language | Is it clearly reflection, not financial, legal, tax, investment, or mental-health advice? |
| 1–4 | Title, note, welcome, support map | Does support language point toward qualified resources rather than replace them? |
| 5–70 | 60 calm-money practices + six practice reviews | Read each prompt. Does it separate facts, feelings, and a next step without telling the reader what to buy/sell/do financially? |
| 71–76 | Six monthly clarity reviews | Does every monthly page remain undated and choice-neutral? |
| 77–168 | Decision notes | Check privacy-sensitive wording, page consistency, and no accidental advice in headers. |

**Specific red flags:** investment/budget directives, savings/debt outcomes, promises to reduce financial anxiety, credit/legal/tax advice, “hypno” or therapeutic audio claim.
