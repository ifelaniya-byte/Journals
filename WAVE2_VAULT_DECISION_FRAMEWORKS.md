## WAVE 2: Open-Slot Approval Framework

**All Wave 2 products require an "open-slot decision" before moving forward.**

### What Is an Open-Slot Decision?

An open-slot decision is a **strategic business approval** confirming:
1. The product aligns with portfolio strategy
2. Resources are allocated for review/approval cycles
3. Market demand justification is sufficient
4. Pricing and positioning are approved
5. Timeline and dependencies are clear

**Not an open-slot decision does NOT mean the product is bad—it means you don't currently have capacity or market validation to push it forward.**
